Cert Roadmap

Certifications can help you get noticed. Many jobs don't require them, but some do, especially government and contractor roles that require Security+ or similar. No cert guarantees a job. Hands-on practice and the ability to explain what you did matter just as much. Free learning comes first. A cert makes sense once you have a target role in mind.

Prices change often. Always check the official page for current cost, exam details, and any discounts for students or veterans.

Stage 1: Foundations (start learning here)

Most of these exams have no formal eligibility requirement, but the vendors still recommend some experience. For example, CompTIA recommends Network+ knowledge and about two years of security or systems administration experience before Security+. You can still study the material from day one.

One example path toward a first SOC job

This is one option, not a required sequence.

  1. Learn networking and Linux basics with free resources (see Hands-on Labs).
  2. Study the Security+ objectives, and take the exam if it fits your goals and budget.
  3. Practice in free labs and build a small portfolio of what you did (see Hands-on Labs).
  4. Apply for help desk, IT support, or junior SOC roles once your skills fit the job requirements (see Getting Hired).
  5. Later, if you want, add CySA+, BTL1, or a tool cert. These are optional and not prerequisites for applying.

Training and exams can cost money, even when you start with free resources.
