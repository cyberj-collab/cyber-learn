How Cyber-Learn works

Start with curiosity, not a certification.

Explore the work first

Cybersecurity includes investigating suspicious activity, helping people use technology safely, managing risk, and building secure systems. See what the roles involve before paying for a course or exam.

Learn at your own pace

You don't need to choose a specialty today. Pick one topic, try one activity, and use what you learn to decide what to explore next.

Practice safely

Use the fictional activities here or a lab that explicitly authorizes what you want to try. A public website or network is not an invitation to test it.

Not sure cyber is for you?

Take the short, no-pressure self-check. There are no wrong answers, just roles worth reading about first.
