Start Here

You don't need to memorize every technical term to get started. Learn one idea, try it in a small example, and read the explanation. Getting something wrong is part of practice.

Suggested learning order

  1. Accounts and identity: passwords, authentication, and multifactor authentication.
  2. Messages and trust: phishing, checking requests, and reporting suspicious messages.
  3. Reading evidence: logs, unusual patterns, and the limits of what a log can prove.

Visitors can skip around. These activities introduce a few tasks; they are not a career assessment or certification preparation course.
