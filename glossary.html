Cybersecurity Glossary

New word? Start here. These short definitions introduce the ideas rather than covering every technical detail.

Asset
Something worth protecting, such as an account, device, or information.
Threat
Something that could cause harm to an asset.
Vulnerability
A weakness that could be exploited or triggered.
Risk
The possibility and consequences of harm, considering factors such as likelihood and impact.
Authentication
Checking that someone or something is who it claims to be.
Authorization
Deciding what an authenticated user or system is allowed to do.
Multifactor authentication (MFA)
Authentication using more than one distinct factor type, such as something you know and something you have; two passwords are not two different factors.
Phishing
A deceptive message or interaction intended to trick someone into revealing information or taking an unsafe action.
Malware
Software intended to perform malicious actions.
Log
A record of events generated by a system or application.
Incident
An event that threatens or compromises security and requires a response under an organization's process.
Least privilege
Giving an account only the access needed for its tasks.
Encryption
Transforming readable data into a protected form that requires the appropriate key to recover it.
Hash
A value calculated from data, often used to check integrity; hashing is not reversible encryption.
Patch
An update that corrects software issues, sometimes including security weaknesses.
