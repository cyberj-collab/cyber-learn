About Cyber-Learn

Cyber-Learn helps people considering a cybersecurity career explore the field, understand the basics, and choose an informed next step. It was designed and built by Jean A. Dumont as a portfolio project.

Jean Dumont is a veteran and former law enforcement officer who is now moving into cybersecurity. He served as a Sergeant in both the U.S. Army and the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve, where he led and mentored teams. He later served as a sheriff’s deputy and a police officer. That work taught him to stay calm under pressure, keep the details straight and communicate clearly.

He holds a B.S. in Criminal Justice and an Associate’s in Criminology and Criminal Justice. He is now studying for a Bachelor’s in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance at Western Governors University and a cybersecurity certificate at Ivy Tech, and he is preparing for CompTIA Security+. He builds hands-on projects in Python, Cisco Packet Tracer and TryHackMe, and he shares his work on GitHub. Jean speaks English, French and Haitian Creole fluently and holds an active Secret clearance.

About this site

Cyber-Learn is a hand-built static website written in plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, with no frameworks or trackers. The source code is on GitHub. Resources are educational; they do not promise certification success or employment.
